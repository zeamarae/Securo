shield Purpose of the System

Securo is designed to elevate campus security and emergency preparedness by uniting stakeholders across a trusted, unified safety platform. The system bridges communication barriers, ensuring that distress signals, safety notifications, and campus updates travel instantly between students on grounds, authorized guardians at home, and safety administrators on duty.

By combining rapid telemetry, geofence boundary awareness, direct support channels, and encrypted evidence documentation, Securo provides transparent, reliable safety protection across daily academic routines and critical emergency scenarios.

verified_user Privacy & Relationship Safeguards

Student privacy is a foundational pillar of the Securo platform. Guardian-to-student links require explicit mutual consent and registered account verification. Location data is shared exclusively under verified safety parameters or during active emergency incidents.

All sensitive records, evidence posts, and messaging logs are securely managed within Firebase cloud infrastructure, governed by strict authentication and role-based access rules.
